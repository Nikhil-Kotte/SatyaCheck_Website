# SatyaCheck trailer, v2 (polish pass)

**What it is:** A check that tells your parents, during the call, whether the voice on the phone is really their son, and shows why.
**For:** Indian families, especially parents on UPI, and the grown-up children who set it up.
**What sets it apart:** Three independent signals (who, how, what) and a warning that quotes its evidence. A synthetic voice alone is not treated as a scam, so a bank's automated call is not flagged.
**Visual hook:** A mother reaches for a ringing phone. "Rahul (Son)" is calling.
**Tone:** `cinematic`, restrained. Dark app theme, blue accent, red only for threat.
**Share caption:** "It's his voice. It isn't him."

## What changed from v1 (from review)
1. Story order: human moment → incoming call → "It's his voice / It isn't him" → brand → analysis → verdict. Poster/frame 0 is the hook line, not the verdict.
2. The red "FAKE VOICE DETECTED" verdict appears once. Analysis stages are restrained (neutral cards, one red number at a time).
3. One trust score, 12/100, and it has settled before anyone can pause on it.
4. Analysis cut to: primary 98% synthetic-voice likelihood, secondary 94% urgent-payment intent, context quote. Model names, tags and advisory card removed.
5. Bank call is generic ("your bank statement"), no real bank, no account number.
6. 2s of human context from story clip 2 up front; transcript now "Maa, …" to match her.
7. Brand: short sting after the reveal, full outro at the end.
8. Two masters: 16:9 (1920×1080) and 9:16 (1080×1920), each composed for its frame. "Runs fully offline" chip replaced with claims the website makes (warns during the call, report to 1930 in one tap).
